# Site observations · Pienvichit Building

Inspected in Google Maps Street View during this session. Capture date shown by Google: May 2019. These observations establish visible forms, not surveyed measurements or current site condition.

## West-side frontage

Position: 16.4724048, 102.8230693. Panorama ID: UoVmUDT-gWDvDSHBTU7UPA.

- Two-way asphalt lane, solid yellow centre line and white edge lines.
- Red/white alternating kerbs.
- Continuous metal-post covered pedestrian path along the building side of the street.
- A raised forecourt edge behind the path, with stainless horizontal rails, drainage strip, flags and concrete picnic tables.
- Hedges on the opposite side of the road.

## Main entrance

Position: 16.4723106, 102.8230672. Panorama ID: V3LH7I12piRwkfpgh78fGQ.

- Broad multi-tread brown/tan stair rises directly from the roadside walkway.
- The entrance is located between the tall glass cylinder and the lower glass cylinder/connecting bridge, toward the lower rotunda.
- The top landing is continuous and carries a simple white name board, potted flowering shrubs and a separate engineering gear display.
- Stainless rails border the sides; a sloped side access is visible in the supplied reference.
- Covered walkway segments stop on either side of the broad stair opening.
- The 2019 name board has no graduation arch. A temporary arch appears in the newer KKU event photo, so it is not included in the default architectural model.

## South approach

Position: 16.4721255, 102.8230635. Panorama ID: QAqk2E_cx4Zp5UQj2dj9fg.

The campus road curves south/east with covered pedestrian paths, lawns, palms, shade trees and parking areas. A crossing is visible near the entrance approach.

## Implementation limits

Step count, dimensions, offsets and terrain outlines were estimated from perspective photographs. The model is not a measured 100% replica. Interior partitions and unspecified rooms remain schematic. Room layouts are outside the current exterior-only scope; the floor selection and cutaway controls have been removed from the interface.


## East facade and open podium — verified in Street View

Position: 16.4724115, 102.8238392. Panorama TFVQgBWMEokbBvqPnalrgw, December 2016.

- Tall pink outer piers frame a two-storey recessed podium, with open public circulation at ground level.
- Mezzanine glazing sits behind the outer columns. It does not extend to the ground across the public hall.
- The narrow east facade is white, with shallow curved balconies, broad paired windows and narrow outer vertical windows.

## Google Maps reviewer photographs

Inspected the available first ten gallery photographs, including June 2019 east elevation by oh san; May/June 2026 photographs by Ladda Tanwanichkul; February 2019 entrance by Jarupol Suriyawanakul; and 2017 exterior photographs by Krittiwakorn Thammasaeng. These are different dates and do not establish a single current surveyed condition.

The supplied close photographs show: a grey-and-tan terrazzo floor grid; inner white square piers; recessed mezzanine glazing; benches in an open hall; a brown steel bridge with two distinct walking decks and braces; and two open white levels below the small glazed rotunda. Reference screenshots are retained in the in-app gallery.

Temporary traffic cones, barriers, road closures, construction materials and event decorations are excluded from the architectural model, as requested. Permanent roads, kerbs, paths, steps, rails and covered walkways remain.

Some images in the first gallery are of neighbouring buildings or a food court. They are not used as evidence for unseen Pienvichit facades. The rear stair elevation is supported by the user's rear photograph; it has not been independently covered in every direction by review imagery. Exact dimensions and all concealed surfaces still require measured drawings or an on-site survey.
